# CASE-002 Public Source Register

[Home](../) · [Source documentation](./) · [Methodology](../methodology.html)

This register covers the evidence cited in the [Marywilska 44 preliminary assessment](../cases/case-002-marywilska-44.html). Dates refer to publication or record dates where known. Court-supplied records are identified by docket, document and PDF page. The register links to public records where available.

<a id="src-004"></a>

### SRC-004 — Polish National Prosecutor’s Office, Marywilska charges announcement

**12 May 2025 · Official investigative statement.** The “Ustalenia” section reports the 11 May filming instruction and anticipated time, describes the fire as arson, and attributes direction to Russian intelligence. The announcement provides the prosecution account. The original instruction, response and underlying forensic reasoning are unavailable. [Official statement](https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/zarzuty-m44)

<a id="src-005"></a>

### SRC-005 — Polish National Prosecutor’s Office, multi-country arson indictment announcement

**2 April 2026 · Official prosecution announcement.** The announcement dates the indictment to 25 March, repeats the advance tasking chronology, alleges filming and onward transmission, and presents the prosecution’s attribution. It may draw on the same investigative material as SRC-004. [Official announcement](https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/akt-oskarzenia-w-sprawie-podpalen-obiektow-wielkopowierzchniowych-na-rzecz-wywiadu-federacji-rosyjskiej)

<a id="src-012"></a>

### SRC-012 — Masovian Provincial State Fire Service, Marywilska response report

**12 May 2024 · Official emergency-response record.** The alarm and response chronology establish the occurrence and response. The alarm time does not establish ignition time or cause. [Fire service report](https://www.gov.pl/web/kwpsp-warszawa/pozar-hali-handlowej-na-ul-marywilskiej-44)

<a id="src-013"></a>

### SRC-013 — Warsaw-Praga District Prosecutor’s Office, investigation-opening announcement

**13 May 2024 · Official procedural announcement.** Records the opening of the investigation with cause still undetermined at that stage. [Investigation announcement](https://www.gov.pl/web/po-warszawa-praga/komunikat-w-sprawie-sledztwa-dot-pozaru-hali-przy-ulicy-marywilskiej-44)

### SRC-014 — Polish National Prosecutor’s Office, completion of scene examination

**2 December 2024 · Official investigative progress report.** Documents completion of scene examination. The underlying cause opinion and its reasoning remain unavailable. [Scene-examination statement](https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/zakonczenie-ogledzin-pogorzeliska-ch-marywilska-44)

<a id="src-017"></a>

### SRC-017 — Polskie Radio RDC, reporting on the expert cause opinion

**12 August 2025 · Reporting on a prosecutor-disclosed expert conclusion.** Reports receipt of the opinion and its conclusion of arson. The underlying opinion and supporting analysis were unavailable. The assessment used indexed article text because direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. [RDC article](https://rdc.fm/aktualnosci/warszawa/pozar-marywilska-przyczyna-opinia-bielglych_XC8fkoPIUlCLNZH1YP7d)

<a id="src-027"></a>

### SRC-027 — Warsaw-Praga Regional Court, BOI.0123.422.2026

**19 November 2026 · Court correspondence, copy held by the researcher.** PDF pp. 1–2 identify the parent indictment docket V K 91/26, confirm the 12 October severance into V K 272/26, and explain why the two closed-hearing orders could not be released. Later docket outcomes and Polish finality or appeal status remain unverified. The post-cutoff correspondence status in the report comes from the researcher’s 24 November update.

<a id="src-028"></a>

### SRC-028 — Warsaw-Praga Regional Court, judgment in V K 118/25

**18 September 2025 · Anonymized judgment, copy held by the researcher.** PDF pp. 2–3 contain the operative findings on organized-group participation and assistance in evading criminal responsibility. Finality and any appeal outcome remain unverified.

<a id="src-029"></a>

### SRC-029 — Warsaw-Praga Regional Court, written reasons in V K 118/25

**Reviewed 20 November 2026 · Anonymized judicial reasons, copy held by the researcher.** PDF p. 3 describes the assignment involving a redacted Warsaw shopping center. PDF pp. 9–14 document the court’s evaluation of evidence, including material from the Lithuanian investigation. PDF pp. 17–18 describe roles and allocation of tasks within the network. The original filming instruction and evidence identifying a specific intelligence service or ignition command remain unavailable.

<a id="src-031"></a>

### SRC-031 — Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, reply to interpellation 18049

**5 August 2026 · Official attribution statement.** The ministry reiterates Russian-intelligence attribution based on prosecution, police and security-service findings. It cites the September 2025 judgment. The conviction scope is documented separately in SRC-028. [Ministry publication](https://www.gov.pl/web/dyplomacja/odpowiedz-na-interpelacje-nr-18049-w-sprawie-pozaru-przy-ul-marywilskiej-44)
