## CASE-001 public source register

This register documents the ten public sources cited in the [Vilnius IKEA assessment](../cases/case-001-vilnius-ikea.html), reviewed for the report’s evidence cutoff of 17 November 2026. Descriptive English entry titles identify the records; they are not presented as verbatim translations of original titles. Passage locators refer to the linked originals. This register adds documentation, not new evidence or revised judgments.

SRC identifiers are stable research references, not a sequence of evidentiary weight. The report cites SRC-002 through SRC-010 plus the ministry reply identified below by its official interpellation number. Gaps in the sequence do not imply missing endnotes. Private correspondence and internal working notes are retained outside this public repository.

Each entry identifies what the source supports and what remains unestablished. Official investigative statements are distinguished from court findings. Publications drawing on a shared investigation or the same statements are not counted as independent corroboration merely because their publishers differ.

## Sources

<a id="src-002"></a>

### SRC-002 — Lithuanian prosecutors: Vilnius IKEA referral announcement

**Publisher / author:** Prosecutor General’s Office of Lithuania  
**Publication / record dates:** 17 March 2025  
**Source character:** Official prosecutorial announcement; Lithuanian with official English text.

[Original source](https://www.prokuraturos.lt/lt/teismui-perduodamas-asmuo-kaltinamas-prekybos-centro-padegimu-ir-dar-vieno-teroristinio-akto-planavimu-with-english-translation/11117)

**Relevant passages:** Official English text: attribution, Warsaw agreement, planning/reporting, device placement and filming, and 13 May detention paragraphs.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports a €10,000 agreement covering attacks in Lithuania and Latvia, reconnaissance, digital coordination, filming and a subsequent BMW reward. Supplies Lithuania’s Russian military/security-interest attribution.

**Limitations:** Investigative account, not the indictment or judgment. Original communications, payment records and statement provenance have not been inspected. Does not establish initial online recruitment, predominant motive, actual cash payment or a specific Russian service’s command.

<a id="src-003"></a>

### SRC-003 — Vilnius Regional Court: IKEA judgment announcement

**Publisher / author:** Vilnius Regional Court  
**Publication / record dates:** 24 November 2025  
**Source character:** Official judicial announcement; Lithuanian.

[Original source](https://vat.teismas.lt/naujienos/teismas-paskelbe-nuosprendi-ikea-padegimo-byloje/1111)

**Relevant passages:** Findings paragraph beginning “Teismas pripažino”; sentencing paragraphs; final appeal paragraph.

**Use in CASE-001:** Authoritative court account of D. B.’s participation in the Vilnius attack and terrorist group, related preparations, and the announced three-year-four-month sentence.

**Limitations:** Announcement, not full written reasons or exhibits. Does not automatically adopt all prosecution details or attribute Russian sponsorship. The announcement alone does not establish subsequent appeal status or the precise entry-into-force date.

<a id="src-004"></a>

### SRC-004 — Polish prosecutors: cross-border arson investigation

**Publisher / author:** Polish National Prosecutor’s Office  
**Publication / record dates:** 12 May 2025 (page date)  
**Source character:** Official prosecutorial investigative statement; Polish.

[Original source](https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/zarzuty-m44)

**Relevant passages:** “Ustalenia” and “Podpalenie sklepu IKEA w Wilnie”.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports Russian-intelligence orders, links between Vilnius and Warsaw activity, reconnaissance from 20 April 2024, remote-capable devices and filming tasking.

**Limitations:** Historical page versions and the first appearance of individual details are unverified. Remote capability does not establish actual activation. Filming the Warsaw fire does not establish responsibility for igniting it. Shared joint-investigation evidence limits independence from Lithuanian accounts.

<a id="src-005"></a>

### SRC-005 — Polish prosecutors: multi-country arson indictment announcement

**Publisher / author:** Polish National Prosecutor’s Office  
**Publication / record dates:** 2 April 2026; indictment filed 25 March 2026  
**Source character:** Official indictment announcement; Polish.

[Original source](https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/akt-oskarzenia-w-sprawie-podpalen-obiektow-wielkopowierzchniowych-na-rzecz-wywiadu-federacji-rosyjskiej)

**Relevant passages:** Opening; “Oskarżeni”; Vilnius section; “Kontynuowanie śledztwa”.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports person-specific allegations, reconnaissance, recording/transmission, cross-border links and Russian-intelligence direction.

**Limitations:** Announcement, not the indictment text or judgment against all accused. Charges differ by person. Does not identify a specific service’s command or authenticate participant communications. May share evidence with earlier official accounts.

<a id="src-006"></a>

### SRC-006 — Lithuanian Police: 9 May 2024 incident summary

**Publisher / author:** Lithuanian Police  
**Publication / record dates:** Record date 9 May 2024; webpage updated 10 May 2024; initial publication time unknown  
**Source character:** Official contemporaneous incident summary; Lithuanian.

[Original source](https://policija.lrv.lt/lt/ivykiu-suvestines/2024-05-09-suvestine/)

**Relevant passages:** “Gaisrai”, first paragraph, concerning Vikingų Street in Vilnius.

**Use in CASE-001:** Records a warehouse fire at approximately 03:55, damage and initial suspicion of arson.

**Limitations:** Does not identify IKEA by name, a participant, a device or foreign involvement. Later sources identify the store. The update marker does not establish when the page first became publicly available.

<a id="src-007"></a>

### SRC-007 — To Build a Fire

**Publisher / author:** The New Yorker; Joshua Yaffa  
**Publication / record dates:** Online 2 February 2026; print issue 9 February 2026  
**Source character:** Investigative journalism; English.

[Original source](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/02/09/to-build-a-fire)

**Relevant passages:** Bullying passage beginning “A Ukrainian man who briefly lived”; later-fear passage beginning “In any case, he claimed”.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports an acquaintance’s account of bullying and D. B.’s fear in a later-assignment context; considered as possible pressure-related context.

**Limitations:** Original statements and their influence on initial acceptance remain unverified. Reported pressure does not establish predominant motive. Overlap with other reporting or investigative evidence is unresolved; publication by a different outlet does not alone supply independent corroboration.

<a id="src-008"></a>

### SRC-008 — Onet: investigative reporting on the Marywilska operation and related actors

**Publisher / author:** Onet; Mateusz Baczyński  
**Publication / record dates:** 8 December 2025  
**Source character:** Investigative journalism based on reported case-file access; Polish.

[Original source](https://wiadomosci.onet.pl/tylko-w-onecie/sluzby-mafia-i-wujek-sasza-ujawniamy-nieznane-fakty-ws-podpalenia-przy-marywilskiej/t9kb5bb)

**Relevant passages:** “Podpalenia”, opening offer/acceptance paragraphs.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports $10,000 per action and acceptance, relevant to comparing compensation accounts.

**Limitations:** Specific underlying provenance of the offer/acceptance passage is unresolved; case files were not obtained. Currency and per-action terms differ from SRC-002’s €10,000 agreement and remain unreconciled. Acceptance does not establish causal motivation or cash receipt.

<a id="src-009"></a>

### SRC-009 — Polish prosecutors: September convictions announced in October 2025

**Publisher / author:** Polish National Prosecutor’s Office  
**Publication / record dates:** 24 October 2025; reported judgment dated 18 September 2025  
**Source character:** Official prosecutorial account of a judicial outcome; Polish.

[Original source](https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/wyrok-skazujacy-trzy-osoby-za-udzial-w-zorganizowanej-grupie-przestepczej-o-charakterze-sabotazowo-terrorystycznym)

**Relevant passages:** Opening outcome paragraphs; nonfinality paragraph before “Ustalenia śledztwa”; distinguish the latter investigative section.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports Warsaw-Praga Regional Court convictions for group participation and, for two defendants, assistance with an escape. Used in assessing the related Polish proceeding and court identity.

**Limitations:** Not the judgment text; no docket or full reasoning obtained. Described as nonfinal at publication; later finality unverified. Investigative details appearing later in the release are not automatically adjudicated findings. Court naming differs from the ministry reply below.

<a id="src-010"></a>

### SRC-010 — Lithuanian authorities: parcel-operation coordinator linkage

**Publisher / author:** Lithuanian Prosecutor General and Criminal Police Bureau  
**Publication / record dates:** 6 March 2026; referral dated 27 February 2026  
**Source character:** Joint official investigative announcement; Lithuanian with official English text.

[Original source](https://prokuraturos.lt/lt/teismui-perduota-byla-del-skirtingose-valstybese-organizuotu-ir-planuotu-ivykdyti-teroro-aktu-with-text-in-english/12174)

**Relevant passages:** English paragraph beginning “It should be noted”; surrounding investigation/coordinator paragraphs.

**Use in CASE-001:** Reports a connection between several parcel-operation coordinators and the Vilnius IKEA attack; contextual support for cross-operation linkage.

**Limitations:** Not an authenticated command chain for the IKEA attack. Parcel-operation recruitment methods, payment methods and devices cannot be transferred to D. B. without specific evidence. Shared investigators or underlying material may limit independence.

<a id="interpellation-13517"></a>

### Ministry reply — Interpellation 13517

**Issuing authority:** Polish Ministry of Justice; prosecution-prepared information  
**Record date:** 4 December 2025  
**Source character:** Official parliamentary correspondence; Polish.

[Original public PDF](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDP3JDT/i13517-o1.pdf)

**Relevant passages:** Page 1, including the response headings Ad 1–Ad 3 and identification of the court.

**Use in CASE-001:** Comparison of the related Polish proceeding, the limited scope of the reported charges and court naming. The reply uses Warsaw where SRC-009 identifies Warsaw-Praga.

**Limitations:** Not the judgment or underlying indictment. The court-name discrepancy remains unresolved pending a docket or judicial record. This source is identified by its official interpellation number; no new SRC identifier has been invented for publication.

## Access and preservation

Links lead to the issuing authority or original publisher. Publisher access restrictions may apply to journalism. Original documents and articles are not reproduced here. Locally retained research copies do not imply public redistribution permission. Link availability and page content can change; the report retains its stated evidence cutoff and historical-page limitations.
