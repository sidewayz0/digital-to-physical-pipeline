# About the Publication

[Home](./)

**The Digital-to-Physical Pipeline** is an independent open-source intelligence research project focused on Russian recruitment, reconnaissance, and hybrid operations in Europe.

Its aim is to develop detailed, evidence-led case studies that separate verified facts, allegations, and analytical judgments. Over time, individual investigations may support carefully bounded comparisons across cases.

The research draws on public legal, government, intelligence, journalistic, and specialist materials. Inclusion of a person, organization, or incident does not imply wrongdoing beyond what cited evidence establishes.

**Independence:** The views and assessments published here are those of the author, not an employer, university, or other institution. This repository does not publish the underlying private working vault.
