# Marywilska 44 Fire: Assessing Deliberate Ignition, Advance Tasking and Russian-Intelligence Direction

**CASE-002 | Preliminary intelligence assessment | Provisionally Assessed — Pending External Records**

**Published 24 November 2026**

## Intelligence question and scope

What does the record establish about the cause of the 12 May 2024 fire at Warsaw’s Marywilska 44 shopping center, D. B.’s advance knowledge, and who directed the operation? This assessment draws on public reporting and court records held by the researcher, reviewed through 20 November 2026. Confidence describes the strength and limits of the evidence for each judgment.

## Key judgments

- **Moderate confidence — Deliberate ignition is the best-supported explanation.** Polish prosecutors describe the fire as arson, and RDC reported that an expert opinion concluded it was deliberately set. The underlying opinion, origin analysis and evaluation of alternative causes remain unavailable. [SRC-004](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-004) · [SRC-017](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-017)
- **Moderate confidence — D. B. likely received the filming assignment before the fire.** Prosecutors date the instruction to 11 May and report that it specified an anticipated time for the fire. Written reasons in a related case describe an assignment to film a Warsaw shopping-center fire whose location is redacted. The date, approximate timing and role support identifying the incident as Marywilska 44. The original instruction remains unavailable. [SRC-004](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-004) · [SRC-005](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-005) · [SRC-029](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-029)
- **Low confidence — Knowing acceptance of a role in a deliberate burning remains unresolved.** The reported timing supports an inference of foreknowledge. The wording of the instruction and D. B.’s response would help distinguish awareness of planned arson from an advance assignment given with limited information. [SRC-004](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-004) · [SRC-029](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-029)
- **Moderate confidence — Russian-intelligence direction through intermediaries is provisionally favored at the operation level.** Polish prosecutors explicitly attribute direction to Russian intelligence. The related court reasons describe recruitment and allocation of tasks by intermediaries, including direction of the network by a Russia-based individual. The specific service and the command link to whoever started the fire remain unresolved. [SRC-004](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-004) · [SRC-005](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-005) · [SRC-029](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-029)

## Evidence and alternatives

The fire service documented the alarm and emergency response on 12 May. The next day, the Warsaw-Praga prosecutor announced an investigation while the cause was still undetermined. A December 2024 release documented completion of the scene examination. Accidental ignition remains possible. The reviewed material supplies no affirmative scene evidence for a specific accidental mechanism. Conditions that accelerated spread could have coexisted with deliberate ignition. [SRC-012](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-012) · [SRC-013](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-013) · [SRC-014](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-014) · [SRC-017](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-017)

An assignment first given after the fire began fits the reported chronology less well because the prosecution dates the instruction to the previous day. The related court reasons give neither the original instruction date nor its wording. The fire-service alarm time marks the emergency response. The precise ignition time and receipt time of the instruction remain unverified. The prosecution separately alleges recording and onward transmission. [SRC-004](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-004) · [SRC-005](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-005) · [SRC-012](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-012) · [SRC-029](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-029)

Autonomous criminal direction has less affirmative support than the official attribution. Overlapping interests and separate chains for filming and ignition remain plausible. The Polish court reasons draw on Lithuanian investigative material, and the Polish foreign ministry’s later statement relies on existing Polish investigative findings. These records may share underlying evidence. The reviewed material leaves the initial recruitment channel and any recurring cross-case model unresolved. ## Proceedings and current status

- **V K 118/25.** On 18 September 2025, the Warsaw-Praga Regional Court convicted three defendants of organized-group participation. Two were also convicted of helping an offender evade criminal responsibility. These convictions concern group and support conduct. Finality and any appeal outcome remain unverified. [SRC-028](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-028)
- **V K 91/26 and V K 272/26.** The Warsaw-Praga Regional Court confirmed the parent indictment docket and that D. B.’s case was severed on 12 October 2026 into V K 272/26. The court withheld the two orders in the closed-hearing minutes. Confirmation of the reported 5 November appearance under the severed docket and its subsequent disposition remains pending. As of 24 November, no email response had been observed to the 20 November follow-up. [SRC-027](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#src-027) · [Hearing report](../references/case-002-marywilska-44.html#hearing-report)

## Records that could change the assessment

- The expert cause opinion or a substantive extract could show the physical basis for deliberate ignition and how alternatives were evaluated.
- The original filming instruction, response, or attributable testimony could clarify timing and what D. B. understood. Original recording and transmission records could help establish what he did.
- Reasoned findings or authenticated communications could clarify who directed the intermediaries and whether that chain extended to ignition.
- Later docket records could confirm the outcome of the severed proceeding and the finality or appeal status of the September 2025 judgment.
