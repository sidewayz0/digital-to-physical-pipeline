## Publication principles

- Separate established facts, allegations, and analyst assessments.
- Use traceable sources, avoid treating derivative reporting as independent corroboration, and identify outstanding primary records.
- Explain confidence, attribution limitations, alternative interpretations, and the date of each assessment.
- Keep private correspondence, working notes, and unpublished administrative material out of this public repository.
- Preserve revisions through Git history and identify material updates to published conclusions.
